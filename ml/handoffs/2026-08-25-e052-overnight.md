## 2 · The night's scientific content, in one paragraph

The bimodal toy (x_{t+1} = x_t ± PATTERN, one coin for the whole field) is
axis B's microcosm, and the measured head-to-head lands on the analytic
targets: the deterministic head is pinned at ratio 0.9939 (conditional mean =
persistence, the blur), while the diffusion head reads per-member ratio 2.0212
against an analytic 2.0 (the conditional-variance tax, exactly as slide 4
claims), ensemble-mean 1.146 (analytic 1.0625 at M=16), fair CRPS 0.4964
against the same head-as-degenerate-ensemble's 0.8854 (analytic perfect 0.4427),
sign coherence 0.9956 against a factorized floor of 0.031 — i.e. the sampler
draws ONE field-wide mode per member, the joint-law property no per-pixel head
can have. The shift toy (eastward roll — a purely spatial law) gives the field
head ratio 0.0081 where a neighbourless per-pixel head is persistence-bound by
construction. Toy numbers verify mechanisms, not levels.
