# E-093 — Pentad-of-year climatology: the mean of every five-day slot of the year over any span of years

**What this is for.** Chris, 2026-10-13: *"Make sure all channels are available
at their granularity. If it's a pentad channel, offer pentad climatology as
well."* E-091 gave every DAILY store a climatology per calendar day. The
five-day global tensor (family 7.2, read by the Data tab through family 10's
registry) holds its channels as five-day means: twenty years of them is about
1,460 maps per channel. E-093 publishes, for the tensor's three gridded groups
(g025, g100, oc025), sums that make any span of years cost two small reads per
slot of the year, the same way E-091 does for days.

## 1. The slot of a bin

Bin `b` covers the five days that start on 1982-01-01 + 5·b days (UTC). Its slot is

```
slot = min(72, floor((day_of_year(bin start) − 1) / 5))        0 … 72
```

So slot `s` holds the bins that **start** on days 5s+1 … 5s+5 of their year,
and slot 72 holds those starting on days 361 … 365, or 366 in a leap year.
Each bin is charged to one slot and to the year of its start. The bins slip one
day against the calendar in every leap year since 1982, so a year normally
gives one bin to a slot but can give 0 or 2. For example, 1984 puts the bins
starting on days 361 and 366 into slot 72, and the counts record that. Nothing
is forced.

The tab's time value for slot `s` is 2000-01-01 + 5·s days, in a nominal leap
year. A slot is filed under the month of that day. That is what the Data tab's
months filter selects on: slot 11 (25–29 February 2000) is a February slot,
and slot 12 (1 March) is a March slot.

## 2. What is published (the contract the reader and the globe layer code against)

Per group, under `tensors/family7_global025_pentad_l2/poy/v1/<group>/` on the
project's Hugging Face dataset, for each slot `ss` = 00 … 72:

- `sum_p{ss}.npy`: `[channel, year, lat, lon]` float32, **in physical units**
  (the tensor is z-scored on disk; each value is de-z-scored as z × sd + mean
  with the index's `norm`, in float64, before it is summed). The file is
  **cumulative over the years**: plane `(c, y)` is the sum of every finite value
  of channel `c` in the bins of slot `ss`, from the record's first year through
  year `y`.
- `count_p{ss}.npy`: the same shape, uint8. It says how many bins that sum holds.
- `manifest_c{kk}.json`: one per job (a chunk of slots). It gives sizes,
  sha256, headers, the years, the slot table with bins per year, and the
  falsifier's numbers.

```
plane (c, y) offset = header_len + (c · n_years + y) · H · W · itemsize
row 0 = the southernmost latitude (lat0 = −90), column 0 = lon0 = −180
mean of a slot over years a…b = (S[b] − S[a−1]) / (N[b] − N[a−1])     (S[a−1] = N[a−1] = 0 when a is the first year)
leaving year e out            : subtract (S[e] − S[e−1]) and (N[e] − N[e−1])
N = 0                         : no value (NaN)
```

A year that has no bin in a slot repeats the plane before it.

## 3. The source

The bin-major `.npy` on the Hub is read with one HTTP range per bin. Each range
is the whole slab, at offset `header_len + (b − bin_first) · H · W · C · 2`.
Every answer must be a 206 with the slab's length and the file's total size in
`Content-Range`. The `.npy` header is read and checked against
`data/family7_index.json` first. NaN means missing and is excluded. A 429 from
the Hub is waited out, as in E-088/E-091.

## 4. The falsifier

It runs in every job, before anything is uploaded. For the first and last slot
of the job's chunk, every native slab is kept. The composed mean is then
compared with numpy's mean of those bins over these spans:

- the whole record
- a 20-year span
- single years at both ends and in the middle
- all but the end years
- the whole record with its middle year left out
- the inner span with another year left out

Counts must match exactly. The mean must lie within ½ ulp of each float32
running total it used — S[b], S[a−1], and both planes of a left-out year —
divided by the count. That is E-091's corrected bound (commit 88015db). If any
check fails, nothing is uploaded.

## 5. How it is built

`ml/export_gridded_poy.py chunk --group G --chunk K` runs on GitHub-hosted
runners, twelve jobs per group. Chunks 0–10 hold six slots each and chunk 11
holds seven. The workflow is `.github/workflows/gridded-poy.yml`: manual
dispatch only, `ubuntu-latest`, and `HF_TOKEN` is its only secret. After the
jobs, `index` reads the twelve manifests per group and writes the site's index.

| group | grid | channels | years | per slot | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| g100: NCEP air–sea fluxes, winds, 2 m air, soil | 1° | 15 | 43 (1982–2024) | 210 MB | ≈ 15 GB |
| g025: GLORYS currents / mixed layer / sea surface height, OISST sea-surface temperature and sea ice | 0.25° | 7 | 43 | 1.56 GB | ≈ 114 GB |
| oc025: OC-CCI ocean colour (chlorophyll, clear-sky coverage) | 0.25° | 2 | 28 (1997–2024) | 0.29 GB | ≈ 21 GB |

The order is g100 first (it is small), verified, then g025 and oc025.

## 6. The reader

`src/f1data.js` adds step `poy`, labelled "Climatology — mean per pentad of
year over the years". It is offered for a bin-major store whose registry file
matches a `complete` store in the index (`configure({gridPoy})`). A 404 for the
index is silent.

The plan works like E-091's:

- The box becomes a band of rows.
- The years and the years left out decide which planes are read.
- The months filter picks the slots by the month of their first day in 2000.
- The days filter is ignored, and the result says so.

The output is a CF climatology with one step per slot, `climatology_bounds`
over the span, and the counts. The sums are already physical, so nothing is
de-z-scored at read time. `scripts/f1data_poy_live_check.mjs` compares the
path with the native maps of the same slot's bins.
