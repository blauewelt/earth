# Archived: technical report v8 (2 September 2026, design section added 16 September) — RETIRED 18 September 2026

This is the last version of the report about the North Atlantic experiments:
*Earth-State Embeddings: a self-supervised codec of the North Atlantic, its
transport read-out, and what a transformer forecasts from it.* It is kept for
the record, is citable as a dated technical report, and is not the current
paper.

**Why it is retired.** Its results section measures a per-pixel codec and a
stage-2 transformer on the North Atlantic 0.25° pentad tensor under the
corrected holdout protocol, and the measurement is a clean negative: the
rolled forecaster beats raw persistence at almost every lead and **beats a
damped-persistence null at none**, and a 200-mode linear inverse model fitted
to the same field is more correlated with the truth from fifteen days to a
year. The transport read-out from frozen embeddings is at parity with the same
head fed raw section values. Those numbers are correct and stay citable from
here; they are no longer the subject of the paper, because the design they
argue for is global rather than regional and has not been trained yet.

**What is carried forward.** Section 4 of this version, *A cone-based design
for the next codec and forecaster*, is the seed of the current paper at
`ml/paper/paper.tex`: the hourglass at two scales, the learnable geometry, the
four-task objective, the queryable decoder and the pre-registered comparisons.
The current paper restates that design from scratch as a design paper for a
global ocean–land–atmosphere model and does not repeat the North Atlantic
results; it cites this report once, as a dated technical report.

**Files.** `paper.tex` / `paper.pdf` (light), `paper_dark.tex` /
`paper_dark.pdf` (dark, generated by `build.sh` — never hand-edited),
`make_figs.py` and `extract_data.py` with `data/report_data.json` (the
