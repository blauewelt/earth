{\LARGE\bfseries Family 1.3 in two pages\par}
\vspace{2pt}{\large where floating plastic is, and how much --- the nets first, the satellites tested against them\par}
\vspace{2pt}{\small blauewelt/earth --- two-page summary of the design note, 11 October 2026. A glossary of every term follows the two pages.\par}
\vspace{4pt}

\textbf{What it is.} Family 1.3 is a proposed set of datasets (``stores'') made of family 1.2 --- the programme's
global ocean and atmosphere measured at 10\,km and 5 days or finer, plus the upper air of the ERA5 reanalysis ---
\textbf{inherited by reference} (listed, not copied), plus a few new stores that say \textbf{where floating plastic
is in the ocean, and how much}. \textbf{What it is for:} a \emph{fifth read-out} beside the programme's four
prediction goals (El Ni\~no, the ocean carbon sink, the Atlantic overturning circulation (AMOC) and sea-surface
temperature (SST)): floating plastic concentration at a place and a time. It is its own family so that family 1.2's
registry and verdicts stay untouched. \textbf{What it is not:} not a satellite plastic map --- open-ocean plastic is
measured almost only by \emph{nets}. A surface net catches pieces down to a third of a millimetre, while the densest
open-ocean microplastic covers about 0.005\,\% of the sea surface (Hu 2021), far below what a 10\,m or 1\,km pixel
can see; satellites see plastic only where it piles up (windrows, river plumes, harbours), and then mixed with
seaweed and foam. Not a pile of compilations either --- the published tables are mostly the same tows compiled
again and again (12,160 of AOMI's 18,860 rows match NCEI on date and position), so each cruise is taken from one
producer, the original first. And not built yet: every source was measured on 2026-10-11; nothing has been
dispatched, rented or uploaded.

\textbf{How it is stored.} One registry, \code{tensors/family1\_3/family13.json} (family code 13, version 1.3) on
the project's public Hugging Face repository, whose \code{inherits\_block} lists the \emph{resolved} chain ---
family 1.gf's 13 stores under their own paths and family 1.2's four --- so a reader opens one parent list, not a
chain (the registry builder needs a small extension for this). The phase-A stores are tier-P point stores built with
family 10's point-store builder: one row per net tow, pump or grab sample, aircraft survey line, identified object or
labelled pixel, with \code{qc} carrying the gear class, object type or label class and \code{platform} the cruise.
Values are stored as the net measured them: pieces/km$^2$ as caught --- NCEI's pieces/m$^3$ is multiplied back by
the net depth each compiler assumed, from a committed per-study table --- with pumps and grabs per m$^3$ in their
own channel and the producer's wind-mixing correction beside the raw value, never in place of it. A row from a later
source is dropped when an earlier one holds the same day within $0.01^\circ$, and every drop is counted. Every value
carries its footprint: a manta tow its swept area and tow line, a pump sample a point, a Sentinel-2 pixel 20\,m.

\gl{AMOC}{The Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation: the Atlantic currents that carry warm water north near the surface and cold water south at depth; one of the four prediction goals.}
