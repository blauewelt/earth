{\LARGE\bfseries Family 1.0.tf in two pages\par}
\vspace{2pt}{\large the land and its coasts, measured finer than 10\,km and 5 days\par}
\vspace{2pt}{\small blauewelt/earth --- two-page summary of the design note, 17 September 2026. A glossary of every term follows the two pages.\par}
\vspace{4pt}

\textbf{What it is.} Family 1.0.tf is a proposed set of datasets (``stores'') that gives the programme's
forecasting model the continents and a 100\,km coastal band at \textbf{10\,km or finer, every 5 days or finer}:
weather stations and balloons, tide gauges, buoys, gliders, flux towers, fire detections, rivers and soil
probes; laser measurements of forests from orbit; daily satellite land temperature, snow and surface colour; a
list of every satellite and aerial image of the land since 1982; and the land-change maps the model should learn
to predict. \textbf{What it is for:} predicting land change --- where forest will be cleared, how much carbon a
canopy holds and how fast it changes, floods, fire, crops, cities --- and giving the four ocean goals (El Ni\~no,
the carbon sink, the Atlantic overturning, sea-surface temperature) the land and coast that border the sea.
\textbf{What it is not:} not an archive of pixels --- the imagery (petabytes a year) is listed, not copied, until a
small learned encoder turns image tiles into tokens; not gap-filled analyses; and not the embedding route, which is
family 0.9.tf. Sources whose licences forbid republishing are built on a private track and never redistributed.

\textbf{How it is stored.} The same design as family 1.gf: a registry listing every store; \emph{point stores}
as column arrays sorted by five-day bin (64-bit time for records before 1914), with a \code{platforms.json} that
keeps each station's elevation; \emph{daily fields} as compressed $256 \times 256$ tiles per five-day bin, read a
few tiles at a time; \emph{catalogues} as one row per scene (time, centre, cloud and valid fraction, sun or radar
angle, area, a sensor code) with a side table of download links. A LiDAR profile's heights and a sounding's
levels are channels. A yearly target is filed at the end of its year, so it is never an input inside that year.
The coastal band ($\pm 100$\,km) is a recorded build setting. Private stores (\code{tide\_private},
\code{river\_grdc}, \code{ismn}, \code{ecad}; NICFI) sit in a private repository and are listed publicly.
