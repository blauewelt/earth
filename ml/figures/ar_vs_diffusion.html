slide 1 / 6 · the incumbent, precisely

What “autoregressive” concretely means here today

One head, run once per pixel per step: it reads the centre pixel’s embedding history plus a 144-point sunflower stencil of neighbours, and regresses the centre’s next embedding. Space is a fixed linear concat (Linear(145·d_z+2 → d_model) per frame); attention runs over time only (K frames). The field at t+1 is assembled from 84,405 independent point predictions, then fed back.

slide 2 / 6 · the moves, concretely

Which parts of our model can move to diffusion — and what each move buys and costs

The time recursion itself is not on the table: rollout stays autoregressive under any head (GenCast rolls 12-hour steps as we roll months) — diffusion can only replace the per-step transition map, and our pipeline offers four distinct places to do it. Each row is one candidate move; the advantages and the costs in that row are the move’s, relative to what we run today.

What stays fixed under every move

The conditioner and the roll. History conditioning on a 720-day span is what E-045 showed the skill lives in, and it feeds moves 1–3 identically; the rollout loop remains autoregressive in time whichever per-step map sits inside it. Neither is a diffusion candidate — there is nothing to sample over history we already observed.

The order we’d take them

2 → 1, with 3 as an optional cheap probe in parallel and 4 parked. Move 2 first because it is readable on today’s scoreboard and its backbone is move 1’s backbone; move 1 once the probabilistic keys exist, so its value is measured rather than penalized. One variable per arm (ml/CLAUDE.md §1).

slide 3 / 6 · the case for

What a diffusion head would buy — each point tied to something measured

1 · Sharp answers where the conditional is broad — i.e. exactly at pentad/daily

The pentad embedding substantially decorrelates in one step (lag-1 ρ ≈ 0.60), so the one-step conditional distribution is wide, and a conditional-mean regressor must answer with a blur — part of what the 0.506 pentad ratio is. A sampler answers with a plausible member instead, and stays on the data manifold when rolled.

measured: ratio 0.072 monthly vs 0.506 pentad · lag-1 ρ≈0.60 (§cadence)

2 · Ensembles — and the programme’s real deliverable is an event probability

“Will the AMOC weaken?” is P(dip below threshold in a window). A deterministic head — measured to replay the calendar in long rolls (§replay) — cannot state that honestly. M samples give dip probabilities, CRPS, and spread–error diagnostics; a collapsed or non-growing spread would have flagged replay by itself, without the six-context experiment it took to find it.

measured: tracking r flat in lead time 0.70/0.68/0.70/0.71 → replay verdict (§replay)

3 · The +0.057 noise result is one denoising step, found empirically

Training the head on inputs noised at its own error scale is the largest single stage-2 gain the programme has (it made a noised 88M beat a clean 205M). That is precisely the mechanism diffusion training formalizes — learn to map corrupted states back to the manifold, at every corruption level, on inputs and outputs. The measured gain is direct evidence the mechanism pays in this system.

measured: +0.057 @88M (E-036), +0.045/+0.050 @205M (E-037), seed-paired

4 · A joint law over multiple points — which no factorized head can supply

Today pixel i and pixel j at t+1 are conditionally independent: coherent structures (an eddy, a front, a dip event spanning the section) are never modelled as units. Any read-out nonlinear across pixels — event detection, extremes, “does the front reach X” — needs the joint law. Marginal means cannot answer it; samples can. This is the precise sense in which the colleague intuition “diffusion predicts multiple points at once” is right.

structural: factorized marginals vs joint samples — not fixable by capacity

5 · The recipe is proven at this exact shape, and we own the hard half

GenCast: latent-free diffusion at 0.25°, 12-h steps, 30-member ensembles, beat ECMWF ENS on ~97% of targets. Stable-Diffusion-style latent DiT: codec = VAE, denoiser attends the latent field. Our codec programme is that VAE, already trained, audited (Tier-1 recon), and published — a diffusion head is stage 2 only, the expensive half already exists.

precedent: GenCast (Nature 2025) · DiT (Peebles & Xie) · latent diffusion

6 · Attendability doctrine, completed

“The embedding’s job is to make the state attendable” — yet stage 2 never attends spatially: space is one frozen Linear. A field DiT is the first stage-2 design that actually attends over the tokens the codec was built to provide, with no dead slots and no hand-chosen stencil geometry.

today: attention over K=24 time tokens only; stencil = fixed concat

slide 4 / 6 · the case against

What it costs — and the two measured pathologies it does not fix

1 · The scoreboard would decide the experiment before the model runs

Forecast ratio and corridor AUC are squared-error scores; their optimum is the conditional mean. For an independent sample, E‖x−ẑ‖² = E‖x−μ‖² + Var — a single diffusion sample scores worse by exactly the conditional variance, i.e. worst at pentad/daily, precisely where its advantage lives. Fair readings are ensemble-mean MSE (costs M passes) and CRPS — neither exists in the eval today. Scoreboard first, or the metric picks the winner.

structural: MSE_sample = MSE_mean + Var — largest where ρ is lowest

2 · Compute: ×NFE ×M on a fleet where probes already dominate

Each rolled step becomes NFE full-field passes, times M ensemble members. Slide 5’s arithmetic says one 30-NFE member is roughly one per-pixel sweep — tolerable — but M=8 members ≈ 8× today’s roll, on a programme where probe cadence was already 65% of daily-arm wall clock and a pentad roll was a ~13 h job (#433).

measured: probes 65% of #400’s wall clock · first pentad AUC roll ~13 h

3 · Determinism is load-bearing here

The gate head reproducing 0.643 eighteen times is the integrity certificate that makes an eval wave readable at all (§3b). A sampler turns “reproduces bit-for-bit” into “reproduces in distribution” unless sampling is seeded and versioned as carefully as the checkpoints — and it adds a genuinely new variance source on top of seed variance, on a programme whose n=1 discipline is calibrated from measured seed pairs.

measured: gate 0.643 ×18 · §3b: replication is bought where variance lives

4 · It does not fix context-span — the axis that actually explained the cadence regression

E-045: at fixed 120-d span, moving the step 5 d → 30 d does almost nothing (0.506 → 0.527); at fixed step, restoring span 240 d → 720 d does almost everything (0.338 → 0.080). That binds the conditioner, whatever the head: a diffusion model conditioned on 120 days of history is starved identically. The 720-day span must be carried into the conditioning — and at K=144 pentad frames that is where the real engineering cost lands (slide 5).

measured: step-only Δ≈0.02 vs span-only Δ≈0.26 (E-045 factorial)

5 · Nor does it abolish replay

Replay is a property of what the data supports plus the objective — a generative head can learn the seasonal analog too, and sample crisp, confident replays. What diffusion adds is the instrument that reveals it (spread that fails to grow), not immunity. The claim must be stated as honesty, not skill.

§replay: long rolls are trajectory replay — the finding survives the head swap

6 · A real new engineering surface, under a 25-input workflow ceiling

Noise schedule, parameterization (ε/v/x₀), sampler and NFE, CFG scale, M, distillation — each a knob that must survive recipe discipline, the input ceiling, and the collapse-guard story (today’s guard reads a pooled probe correlation; a sampling head needs its own health instrument before it can be watched at all). None of temporal.py’s training loop is reusable for the sampling path.

house rules: 25-input hard ceiling · recipes-not-hand-assembly · guards need calibrated instruments

slide 5 / 6 · the concrete design

A latent-field DiT for this system — and the cost arithmetic

This is textbook latent diffusion with our codec as the (already trained, already audited) VAE: denoise the whole next latent field, conditioned on a temporal summary of the history. Factorize time first — the per-pixel temporal machinery we already have is the right conditioner shape — and let the denoiser attend over space, which stage 2 has never done.

Cost arithmetic — design reasoning, not a measurement

Today’s rolled step: 84,405 forwards × ~10¹⁰ FLOPs (24-token seq, 1024×16) ≈ 8×10¹⁴ — because every pixel re-encodes its 145-slot neighbourhood, each z is touched ~145 times.

DiT pass over 5.3k tokens at d 1024, 24 blocks ≈ 6×10¹² → 30-NFE member ≈ 2×10¹⁴ per step. One member is cheaper than today’s sweep; the ensemble (M=8) is ~2× it; a 4-step distilled sampler makes even the ensemble cheaper than today.

Where the real design risk sits

The conditioner, not the denoiser. E-045 says skill lives in the 720-day span; at pentad that is K=144 frames per token column. Full spatiotemporal attention (144 × 5.3k tokens) is out of reach — so time must be factorized into c first (per-patch temporal encoder, exactly the shape of today’s head), and that component inherits the entire context-span question unchanged. The denoiser is standard; the conditioner is ours.

slide 6 / 6 · recommendation

Buy the axes separately: a staged path, with falsifiers

0Scoreboard first — code only, no GPU

Add to the eval before any new head runs: ensemble-mean forecast ratio (comparability with every archived number), CRPS, spread–error ratio, and a dip-event Brier score on the transport read-out. Without these the comparison is decided by the metric, not the model (slide 4.1).

1The clean ablation: joint deterministic field transformer (slide 2’s move 2)

Same DiT backbone, one-shot regression of the whole next field, no sampling — scored on today’s scoreboard against the stencil head at matched parameters, pentad cadence, 720-d-span conditioner. Falsifier: if joint spatial attention ≈ per-pixel stencil-concat, the geometry move buys nothing here, the 145-slot concat was never the bottleneck, and the diffusion case must rest on the sampling move alone. Either answer is cheap and reshapes the question.

2Generative head on the winning backbone (slide 2’s move 1)

Flow matching or EDM on the stage-1 backbone, few-step sampler, M≈8, seeded sampling. Read: ensemble-mean ratio must ≈ stage 1 (sanity: no MSE tax after averaging), CRPS and dip Brier must beat stage 1’s degenerate distribution, spread–error ≈ 1. Falsifier: if the ensemble mean matches but CRPS/Brier do not improve on the deterministic head + a trivial noise ensemble, the distribution head is not earning its NFE.

The one-line verdict

Diffusion is preferable when the deliverable is a distribution over futures — dip probabilities, extremes, multi-point coherence — and a tax when the deliverable is one-step conditional-mean MSE. The colleague intuition is right about the destination; the way to make it survive this repo’s discipline is to buy the three axes separately and let stage 1 tell us whether the joint field or the distribution is doing the work.

Cheapest immediate probe (zero new architecture)

The +0.057 noise result already trains the head at ONE corruption level. Conditioning today’s head on a noise-level embedding and training across a σ-ladder is a two-line change that tests the core diffusion mechanism (multi-level denoising) inside the existing head, on the existing scoreboard, before any DiT is built.
