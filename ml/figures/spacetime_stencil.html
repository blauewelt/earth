slide 1 / 2 · the idea

The stencil samples a slab; the ocean fills a cone

Today every stencil slot is read at every past frame — a dense K×145 rectangle in spacetime. E-045 measured that context span is the axis that matters and registered the seasonal analog as its mechanism — so most of that rectangle is plausibly redundant, and span can only be bought by raising K. The proposal: give each slot its own (Δx, Δy, Δt) — the sunflower taken into the time dimension — so span and cost decouple.

Span is the axis; K is how we pay for it

With span fixed at two years the one-step ratio is flat in step size (0.0721 · 0.0804 · 0.0820 at 30/10/5-day steps); cut the span to 120 d and even 30-day steps read 0.53. Under the slab, two years at pentad means K=144 — 20,880 samples per window, attention 36× the K=24 head, a 10.95 GB monitor batch that OOMed a v5e chip.

E-045 factorial · E-051 launch log

The mechanism is the analog — make it explicit

E-045's registered mechanism for span is the seasonal analog: a 720-day window contains last year's same-calendar frame. If that is what span buys, ~20 pinned samples at −1y/−2y carry the value of ~120 frames of slab — testable for about $4 (arm A1, slide 2).

Information travels; the slab pretends it doesn't

A neighbour at distance r is informative about the centre's future mostly near lag Δt ≈ −r/c (advection: boundary cores ~100–200 km/day, interior Rossby waves ~3–5 km/day at 26°N). The slab samples the whole rectangle; the cone form samples the inflow trajectories. c is measured, not picked: the space-time cross-correlation ridge of the published Z (E-053.0, $0, CPU) — and if no single c exists, that datum chooses the agnostic ball form (“equally distributed in time”) over the cone.

What stays fixed

Target step, persistence baseline, holdout discipline, the frozen codec: only the context sampling moves, so every number stays comparable to the pentad archive (unlike overlapping windows, which change the question). All Δt ≤ 0 — causality untouched.

costSpan decoupled from K

Samples per window = 145·Keff; attention ∝ Keff². Uniform K=24 → 120 d at 1×. Uniform K=144 → 720 d at 36×. A2 → 720 d at 1×. A3 → 3,650 d at 1.8× — a ten-year context for a twentieth of E-051's attention cost, which is what makes decadal span affordable at all.

dangerAnalog pins are a lookup machine

Handing the head last year's frame is replay by construction. The E-043b-PHASE battery (six context-end rolls; flat within-record tracking = replay) is mandatory before any E-053 roll number is quoted. A one-step win with a flat lead-time profile is memorisation sharpened, not forecasting — and is reported as such.

4th dimDepth (and height) join as coordinates

At the head, unification is just coordinates: E-053.2's tokens carry an encoding of (Δx, Δy, Δt); a fourth signed vertical slot (−depth below the surface, log-spaced like the Argo levels; +height for a future atmospheric tier) costs nothing to reserve now. The codec's decoder already answers coordinate queries (channel, Δlon, Δlat, Δmonth) — the system is closer to coordinate-native than it looks.

But depth is today a channel axis compressed into one z per pixel-bin: per-depth tokens are an E-047-class codec redesign (token count × levels, the dz economics redone) — a separate decision, never a head knob.
